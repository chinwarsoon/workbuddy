C2 Submission Flow & Delay — Time-River Sample

Two design variants for the C2 Stacked view. X = month (time). Bands = submission status. Toggle a status in the legend to include or hide it. Hover a month for the breakdown.

Counting rule: each band height is the count of submission records (rows) in that month, grouped by status. Counts are per month only — not cumulative, and not distinct Document No. (distinct-document counting is reserved for the KPI cards). A document with several revisions counts once per row. The NFS band is the only MDL-sourced band: it counts planned documents by their planned first-issue month, not a submission date.

Variant 1 — Stacked area (baseline at 0)

Lower bank = zero baseline. Upper bank = top of stack = monthly total. The total is always readable.

Recommended for status counts: one bank is the zero baseline, so the river height = volume.

Variant 2 — Centered streamgraph

Bands flow symmetrically around a centerline. Stronger "river" look, but the absolute total is hidden (only relative thickness shows).

Use for shape only. The two banks (top and bottom) are both wavy; no flat baseline.
